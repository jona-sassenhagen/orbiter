Write a little web app game.

It's for touch displays. Double touch at a minimum.

There is a small particle that flies in a straight line. When the player touches a point, an orbital attractor appears there. When the particle appears in its orbit, it will be captured. It will then take on speed proportional to the size of the attractor. The attractor will grow in size the longer the player touches that point. When the attractor disappears, the particle will be released and fly away - potentially accelerated. The speed of the particle is indicated by its color.

There is a goal: a bar in one of the corners. The goal needs to be hit with a particle of a certain speed. The color of the bar equals the required speed as indicated by the color of the particle.

If the particle hits anything (borders, obstacles), it is destroyed and the player loses a life. Sole exception: if the particle hits the goal, and is over the speed limit.
First level is without obstacles and with a low speed limit particle.
