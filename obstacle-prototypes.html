Obstacle Prototypes

Four visual directions for Orbiter obstacles. Each one keeps collision geometry simple, keeps destructible obstacle color tied to required speed, and treats indestructible obstacles as pale structural material.

Cut Metal Neon

Faceted plates with a glow shell and a few surface cuts. Keeps the current polygon idea but makes it look intentional.

red gate amber gate solid

Ion Glass

Transparent single-color bodies with specular streaks. Softer and prettier, but still reads as a barrier.

easy hard solid

Orbital Pylons

More industrial and readable at speed. Corners, anchor dots, and heavy silhouettes make late-level clutter cleaner.

medium hard solid

Solid Monoliths

Bold single-color polygons with dark incisions. Most legible, least fussy, and probably cheapest in canvas.
